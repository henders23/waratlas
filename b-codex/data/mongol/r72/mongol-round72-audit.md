# Mongol denominator R72 amendment

R72 preserves every one of the 109 frozen R71 candidate IDs and adds four city events. It has **113 candidates: 107 include, five merge, one superseded**. Every merge target now resolves to an included canonical ID. The R71 CSV and its archive remain unchanged.

## Date reconciliation

Five `date_label` fields now agree with the historical reconciliation already recorded in their R71 reasons: Wulahai **1209**, Dali **1253–1254**, Sambyeolcho **1270–1273**, the second Đại Việt invasion **1285**, and Champa **1282–1284**. Only those five date fields were edited on otherwise unchanged R71 rows.

## Khwarazm and Khorasan split

The R71 `urgench-nishapur-herat` candidate merged into a canonical ID that did not exist. It also joined separate operations at three cities. R72 retains that candidate as `superseded`, with no canonical ID or map point, and records four included events:

| Event | Primary locator | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gurganj (Urgench), 1221 | Juvayni, *History of the World-Conqueror*, Boyle trans., I, ch. XIX, pp. 122–128 | City locality only; no exact siege works or casualty total. |
| Nishapur, 1221 | Juvayni, I, ch. XXVIII, pp. 168–178 | City locality only; no exact siege works or casualty total. |
| Herat, first capture, 1221 | Sayf Heravi, *Tārīḵ-nāma-ye Herāt*, pp. 66–72, as cited in [*Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Herat iii”](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/herat-iii/) | Separate from the later recapture. |
| Herat, recapture, 1222 | Sayf Heravi, pp. 73–93, as cited in [*Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Herat iii”](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/herat-iii/) | Recapture after renewed resistance; no precise day or casualty count. |

Juvayni’s [volume I scan](https://archive.org/details/historyoftheworl011691mbp) supports the Gurganj and Nishapur locators. His editor notes at p. 151 n. 5 that Juvayni does not give a detailed Herat capture account. The Herat details above rely on *Iranica*’s cited account of Sayf; Sayf’s original was not independently inspected. [*Iranica*’s Jalal al-Din entry](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/jalal-al-din-kvarazmsahi-mengbirni/) independently places Gurganj’s investment in 1221. [Its Herat entry](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/herat-iii/) separates Tolui’s initial capture from Eljigidei’s 1222 recapture. These references support locality markers, not siege geometry. No reported medieval casualty totals are reproduced.

R72 is an amendment to the fixed discovery denominator, not a claim that all possible Mongol events have been found. Source prose remains citation-only; atlas descriptions are original synthesis.
